How the tea outturn calculation works

Outturn expresses the weight of tea produced as a percentage of the green leaf weight received. Use weights from the same batch and keep the unit consistent.

Net outturn (%) = Made tea weight ÷ Green leaf weight × 100
Gross outturn (%) = Fired tea weight ÷ Green leaf weight × 100
Green leaf needed = Target made tea weight ÷ (Expected outturn ÷ 100)
Green leaf : made tea ratio = Green leaf weight ÷ Made tea weight : 1

The net and gross labels describe the stages selected above. Factories may record production stages and refuse differently, so confirm that each input matches your own production records before comparing results.

Why there is no single default outturn rate

Published industry figures are useful context, not a universal setting. For example, the Tea Board of Kenya's green-leaf quality guideline describes a seasonal conversion range of 20% to 24% depending on weather. A Kenya tea cultivation manual gives 22.5% as an average example, while the Sri Lanka Tea Board's 2022 annual report describes using a 4.65:1 green-leaf-to-made-tea ratio for its reporting calculation.

These figures come from different contexts and should not be treated as interchangeable factory targets. Use your own recent batch records or the rate supplied by your factory or tea authority.

Tea Outturn Calculator FAQs

What does tea outturn mean?

Tea outturn is the conversion of green leaf weight into made tea weight, commonly expressed as a percentage or as a green-leaf-to-made-tea ratio.

How do I calculate net outturn?

Divide the made tea weight by the green leaf weight from the same batch, then multiply by 100. For example, 225 kg of made tea from 1,000 kg of green leaf is 22.5% net outturn.

What is the difference between net and gross outturn?

This calculator reports net outturn from the made-tea weight and optional gross outturn from the fired-tea weight you enter. Production-stage names and refuse accounting can differ between factories, so use your own records' definitions.

Can I estimate green leaf required for a planned production target?

Yes. Enter the target made-tea weight and your expected net outturn percentage. The calculator divides the target weight by that outturn fraction.

Does the calculator determine green leaf payment or official settlement?

No. It only calculates weight conversion and production estimates. It does not calculate farmer payments, statutory price-sharing, tax, quality grades or regulatory settlements.
