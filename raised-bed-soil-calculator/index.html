Raised Bed Soil Calculator

Plan several raised beds in one project. Enter each bed's inside dimensions and target fill depth, then calculate the volume, bags and optional cost for every ingredient in your own soil-mix recipe.

Your recipe, your measurements: Enter mix percentages from a recipe or supplier you have chosen. This calculator works out quantities; it does not recommend a universal soil recipe, fertilizer rate or plant-specific growing treatment.

1. Choose measurement units

Choose the units for bed dimensions. Ingredient package volumes are entered in litres using the volume printed on the actual bag.

2. Add your raised beds

Measure inside the bed, not the outside frame. For a round bed choose Circle. For an L-shaped or irregular bed, split it into separate rectangles and add each one.

How the raised-bed soil calculator works

For each bed, the calculator finds the inside area and multiplies it by the new fill depth. Any existing fill depth you enter is subtracted from the target depth. Matching beds are multiplied by their quantity, then the volumes are combined.

New mix volume = bed area × (target fill depth − existing fill depth) × number of matching beds
Ingredient volume = total new mix volume × ingredient percentage ÷ 100
Whole bags = ingredient volume ÷ labelled bag volume, rounded up

Rectangular beds use length × width. Circular beds use π × radius². For L-shaped or irregular beds, divide the floor area into non-overlapping rectangles and add them as separate beds. All dimensions are converted to a common unit before totals are calculated.

Use your own recipe and product labels

Soil blends vary by project, growing method, local materials and supplier. Use a mix ratio supplied by your chosen recipe, extension resource or supplier. This tool does not decide which soil ingredients are appropriate for a crop, prescribe fertilizer or calculate soil chemistry.

Package volumes vary. Read the actual label and enter the stated volume in litres; the calculator uses that volume to estimate whole packages. Optional prices are user-entered and are not currency conversions or live supplier quotes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate more than one raised bed?

Yes. Add each bed separately, including its shape, inside dimensions, fill depth and number of matching beds. The summary combines the volumes and ingredient amounts for the full project.

Can I use a different soil-mix ratio?

Yes. Change the ingredient names and percentages to match the recipe you have chosen. The percentages must total 100%. The example values are only a starting example from the cited source, not a universal recommendation.

Does the calculator include soil already in the bed?

Enter the usable existing fill depth for each bed. The calculator subtracts that depth from your target fill depth and estimates only the additional mix you need.

Does it recommend fertilizer or the best soil for my plants?

No. It is a volume and purchasing calculator. Follow suitable local growing guidance, a soil test and the instructions for the exact products you intend to use.
