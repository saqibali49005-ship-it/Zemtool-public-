Time Calculator

Calculate time between clock times or dates, work hours after breaks, time expressions, or add and subtract durations. View clear results in practical time units and decimal hours.

Add or subtract time

Add or subtract multiple durations

Enter one duration per row. Choose whether to add or subtract each row, then calculate the combined total. Hours can exceed 24.

Calculate work hours

Find a shift's gross hours, deduct unpaid break time, and optionally round start and end entries. Break time is entered as one total in minutes.

Optional rounding is applied to the start and end entries before break deduction. This is an arithmetic setting, not payroll or legal-compliance advice; use the rules that apply to your workplace.

Calculate time between dates and times

Enter both local date-time values to calculate the actual elapsed duration, including intervals longer than one day.

Add or subtract time from a date

Move a local date and time forward or backward by days, hours, minutes and seconds. In this mode, one day means 24 elapsed hours.

Date and time fields use your browser/device local time zone. Nonexistent clock times during daylight-saving changes are rejected. Repeated times when clocks move back follow your browser's date handling. This tool does not convert between time zones or count workdays/holidays.

Time expression calculator

Add, subtract, multiply or divide durations with a safe time-expression parser. Use d (days), w (weeks), h (hours), m (minutes) or s (seconds). Multiplication and division use a number as the factor.

Examples: 1d 2h + 45m - 10s, 3h 43m 11s * 6, 3 weeks / 5. A week is 7 days. Calendar months and years are not supported because their lengths vary. Expressions do not execute code or accept parentheses.

What the results mean

Duration results show days, hours, minutes and seconds, total minutes, total seconds and decimal hours. Work Hours separates gross shift time, break time and net time. Date & Time uses actual elapsed time between local date-time values. Expression mode can display a result in seconds, minutes, hours or days.

Example: 9:30 AM to 5:15 PM = 7 hours 45 minutes = 7.75 hours.

How time calculations work

Clock calculations use 60 seconds per minute and 60 minutes per hour. Clock-only duration mode compares two times on a 24-hour clock; select the next-day option when the interval crosses midnight. Date & Time mode compares local date-time timestamps, so daylight-saving transitions can make a calendar day shorter or longer than 24 elapsed hours.

Work Hours calculates the time between the entered shift start and end, applies optional start/end rounding, then deducts the total break minutes. Expression mode converts each unit to seconds before applying addition, subtraction, multiplication or division by a scalar. One week equals seven days; calendar months and years are not treated as fixed-duration units.

Examples: elapsed time, adding and subtracting time

Use the Time Duration mode as an elapsed time calculator when you know a start and end clock time. Choose Add / Subtract Time when you want to move a base clock time forward or backward by a duration.

Overnight elapsed-time example: Start at 10:45 PM and finish at 6:15 AM the next morning. Select the next-day option to calculate 7 hours 30 minutes, or 7.5 decimal hours.
Time addition example: Add 2 hours 30 minutes to a base time of 10:30 AM. The result is 1:00 PM (13:00).
Time subtraction example: Subtract 1 hour 45 minutes from 1:15 AM. The result is 11:30 PM on the previous day.
Work-hours example: A 9:00 AM–5:00 PM shift with a 30-minute break gives 7 hours 30 minutes or 7.5 decimal hours.
Date-time example: From March 7, 2026 at noon to March 8 at noon in New York is 23 elapsed hours because daylight-saving time begins during that interval.
Expression example: 3h 43m 11s * 6 equals 22 hours 19 minutes 6 seconds.

Date & Time values use the browser/device local time zone. This page does not convert between different time zones.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the time between two times?

Enter the start and end clock times and calculate. When the interval crosses midnight, use the next-day option if the end clock time is earlier than the start time.

What is the duration from 9:30 AM to 5:15 PM?

It is 7 hours 45 minutes, which is 7.75 decimal hours.

Why do minutes not work like decimal numbers?

An hour contains 60 minutes, not 100. Therefore 2 hours 30 minutes equals 2.5 hours, while 2 hours 45 minutes equals 2.75 hours.

Can the calculator handle times across midnight?

Yes. Enable the next-day option in the duration calculator when the end time belongs to the following day.

Can I calculate time between calendar dates?

Yes. Open Date & Time, enter a start and end date with their local clock times, then calculate the elapsed duration. The browser/device local time zone is used; cross-zone conversion is not included.

Can I use this as a time addition calculator?

Yes. Select Add / Subtract Time, enter a base clock time, choose Add or Subtract, and enter the hours, minutes and seconds. The result shows the new clock time and whether it falls on the previous or next day.

Can I deduct lunch or rest breaks from work hours?

Yes. Open Work Hours, enter the shift start and end, and enter the total break time in minutes. The result shows gross shift time, the break deduction, net time and decimal hours. You can also subtract individual break durations in Add / Subtract Durations.

Can I add or subtract multiple time durations?

Yes. Open Add / Subtract Durations, enter hours, minutes and seconds in each row, choose Add (+) or Subtract (−), then calculate the total. You can add or remove rows as needed.

Can the total be longer than 24 hours?

Yes. Multiple durations can total more than one day. The result shows the full hours:minutes:seconds duration, the day/hour breakdown, total minutes and seconds, and decimal hours.

Can I calculate a duration across several days?

Yes. Use Date & Time and enter both calendar dates and clock times. This mode reports elapsed time across multiple days.

Can I add or subtract time from a date and time?

Yes. In Date & Time, enter a starting local date/time, choose Add or Subtract, and enter days, hours, minutes and seconds. One day is treated as 24 elapsed hours.

Can I calculate a time expression or multiply a duration?

Yes. Expression mode accepts duration units d, w, h, m and s with addition and subtraction. You can also multiply or divide a duration by a number, such as 3h 43m 11s * 6. Division by zero and ambiguous or unsupported expressions are rejected.

Can I round work start and end times?

Work Hours offers optional nearest 5-, 6- or 15-minute rounding. The selected rounding is applied to both entered shift times before the break deduction. Check your workplace rules before using rounding for payroll.

Method & reference notes

Clock-only modes operate on a 24-hour clock. Date & Time modes use the browser/device local time zone and actual elapsed timestamps; nonexistent local clock times during daylight-saving gaps are rejected, while repeated times follow the browser's date handling. Date-time offsets treat one day as 24 elapsed hours. Cross-time-zone conversion, workday/holiday counting, calendar months/years and leap-second calculations are not included. Work Hours rounding is a calculation option, not a statement of payroll-law compliance.
